      Is a Cold Chain Needed?

      For long-distance distribution and storage, apples benefit strongly from refrigerated handling because low temperature slows respiration, moisture loss, softening and decay. Very short local distribution may tolerate simpler handling, but time and temperature must still be controlled.

      Product Quality from Stakeholder Perspectives

      Grower

      Yield, colour, size, disease-free fruit, high pack-out and return per hectare.

      Wholesaler

      Uniform lots, predictable storage life, low shrinkage and dependable supply.

      Trader

      Specification compliance, delivery reliability, export fitness and low claims.

      Retailer

      Appearance, shelf life, sell-through, low waste and customer satisfaction.

      Consumer

      Taste, sweetness, crispness, freshness, safety, convenience and value.

      LiDO Alignment

      How this platform's architecture maps onto LiDO — Laimburg Integrated Digital Orchard — as documented in Laimburg's own official sources.

      LiDO by the numbers

      Opened October 2022 · ~40 partner companies, universities and startups · ~1,100 visitors over three Open Days · ERDF co-funded · central InfluxDB time-series data platform with API, dashboards and tokened access.

      The two field labs

      Apple orchard: 0.65 ha, Rosy Glow Pink Lady®, flat multi-axis Guyot canopy built for cameras and robots.
      Vineyard: 0.4 ha Chardonnay, slopes up to 70%, used for worker-safety and mechanisation trials.

      Top priorities, next 5–10 years

      Laimburg's own strategy ranks two priorities "very high": climate adaptation & water management, and AI/robotics/plant-level precision agriculture — the same territory this platform targets.

      Where this platform fits LiDO's own roadmap: LiDO's stated 2026 focus is the geolocation of individual trees and vines — giving each plant a fixed digital identity. Laimburg's own documentation describes this as a likely foundation for orchard and vineyard digital twins, where every plant carries its location, images, growth, water status, treatments, yield and quality. That is exactly the layer this platform is built to provide — this isn't an outside idea being pitched to LiDO, it lines up with LiDO's own next step.

      Already validated in the field at LiDO

      Aigritec's robotic harvester and a precision blossom-thinning robot have both been field-tested in the Laimburg orchards — real precedent for the kind of AI-guided field intervention this platform's Scenario Lab models (see the Flower thinning window preset).

      A proven validation pathway

      Printed plant sensors from the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano followed lab → climate chamber → LiDO orchard → prototype improvement before being trusted in the field — the same lab-to-field discipline this platform's Research tab is built around.
